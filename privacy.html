Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Drecot Consults (RC: 6904955), organizer of the HTEA Osun 2027 ("we", "us", or "our"), is committed to protecting the privacy of our vendors, sponsors, nominees, and attendees. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or register for the event:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and company details.
  • Business Information: CAC registration details (for vendors and sponsors), staff names, and job titles (for the HTEA Skills Challenge).
  • Payment Information: Bank transfer details (we do not store card information on our servers).
  • Media: Photographs and videos taken at the event.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process your registration or sponsorship package.
  • Verify staff eligibility for the HTEA Top Hands Challenge.
  • Send you event updates, schedules, and important announcements.
  • Promote the event through our official media channels.
  • Comply with legal obligations in Nigeria.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Our event partners (e.g., HospitalityRecruit) strictly for recruitment and verification purposes.
  • Government agencies (e.g., the Ministry of Culture and Tourism) if required by law.
  • Media partners for event coverage.

4. Photography & Videography

By attending HTEA Osun 2027, you consent to being photographed or recorded. These images may be used on our website, social media, and press releases for promotional purposes without compensation.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at drecotconsults@gmail.com.
