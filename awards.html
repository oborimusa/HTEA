The Excellence Awards

We don't rank businesses as "Best vs. Best." We celebrate the standards of excellence that keep Osun's hospitality industry running.

The 8 Hotel Excellence Awards
These awards celebrate the owners and operators who have built the industry, created jobs, and maintained standards in Osun State.
Zone Award

Excellence Award - Osogbo Zone

Recognizing the leading hotel/restaurant in the capital city that exemplifies service excellence, community impact, and hospitality standards.

Zone Award

Excellence Award - Ife Zone

Honouring the best in the historic city of Ile-Ife, celebrating businesses that serve both locals and tourists with distinction.

Zone Award

Excellence Award - Ilesa/Ede Zone

Acknowledging outstanding hospitality businesses in the Ilesa, Ede, and Ikirun axis for their contribution to the state's growing tourism map.

Property Award

Excellence Award - Boutique & Lodges

Celebrating the intimate, high-quality guest houses and boutique lodges that provide personalized and memorable guest experiences.

Culinary Award

Culinary Excellence Award

For the restaurant or catering business that delivers outstanding food quality, hygiene, and a true taste of Osun's local and continental cuisine.

Lifestyle Award

Nightlife & Entertainment Excellence

Recognizing the lounges, bars, or clubs that provide safe, vibrant, and world-class entertainment experiences for guests and residents.

Tourism Award

Tourism & Travel Excellence

Honouring the tour operators, travel agencies, or transport companies that make it easy for people to explore the beauty of Osun State.

Legacy Award

Lifetime Service Excellence

A special recognition for an elder or pioneer who has dedicated decades to building the hospitality industry in Osun State.

👑 The Top Hand Skills Challenge

This is not a business award. This is a test of individual skill. Professionals and Students will compete live on stage in cooking, housekeeping, and F&B service. One single winner will be crowned the Face of Osun Hospitality 2027 and represent the state at the National Finals in Abuja.
