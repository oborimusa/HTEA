Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE 2026

A live hospitality skills challenge. Real professionals. Real guest scenarios. Live audience. No script. No rehearsal. Just skill.

Apply to Compete See How It Works

Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE is not a beauty pageant. It is a professional skills challenge that selects the two individuals who will represent Kwara's hospitality and tourism sector for the next 12 months.

Competitors are drawn from hotels, restaurants, and tourism operators across the state. They are tested on three pillars — Skill, Craft, and Character — through a live cooking challenge at the Food Fair and a series of real-world guest scenarios on Awards Night.

The winners become official HTEA Hospitality Ambassadors, appearing at partner events, press features, and the HTEA Awards Gala.

Three Pillars
Skill → Craft → Character. Every competitor is judged across all three.
01

🔥 The Heat

Cooking Challenge

Competitors receive a sealed basket of local Kwara ingredients and must create a dish using at least 70% of the basket — under time pressure, on stage, in front of a live audience.

Where: Food Fair
Time: 45–60 minutes
Weight: 30% of total score
02

🎨 The Art

Presentation & Storytelling

Each competitor plates their dish, sets a proper place setting, and delivers a 30-second story about their creation — its name, its meaning, and where its ingredients come from.

Where: Food Fair
Time: 15 minutes
Weight: 20% of total score
03

❤️ The Heart

Real Guest Scenarios

On Awards Night, competitors face three live scenarios — each in the uniform of a different hospitality department. No script. No rehearsal. They must respond in real time.

Where: Awards Night
Time: 5 minutes per competitor
Weight: 50% of total score
The Three Live Scenarios
Each uniform tells a story — Welcome, Comfort, Experience.
🛎️
First Scenario

Front Office — The Guest's Welcome

Competitor wears a suit or blazer. Judges test knowledge of reservations, check-in etiquette, complaint handling, and first impressions — under live pressure.

"A guest arrives at 2 AM. Their booking was cancelled by a system glitch. The hotel is fully booked. What do you do?"
🛏️
Second Scenario

Housekeeping — The Guest's Comfort

Competitor wears a housekeeping uniform or apron. Judges test knowledge of room standards, hygiene, safety protocols, and handling sensitive guest situations.

"You enter a room to clean. The guest is crying — they've just received bad news. What do you do?"
🍽️
Third Scenario

Food & Beverage — The Guest's Experience

Competitor wears service attire. Judges test the service sequence, menu knowledge, allergy handling, upselling, and service recovery.

"A guest complains loudly in the lobby that their food was cold. Other guests are watching. What do you do?"

How Winners Are Chosen

Character cannot be faked. That's why The Heart carries the most weight.

Pillar Focus Weight
🔥 The Heat Technique, taste, use of local ingredients 30%
🎨 The Art Plating, table setting, storytelling 20%
❤️ The Heart Composure, problem-solving, empathy, communication 50%

Eligibility Requirements

You Must

  • Be aged 21–40
  • Be currently employed in the hospitality or tourism sector
  • Work at a hotel, restaurant, tour operator, or related business in Kwara State
  • Provide a letter from your employer confirming employment
  • Be available for all competition phases
  • Have no criminal record

You Cannot

  • Be a current titleholder of another hospitality competition
  • Be an employee of HTEA or KWASHATE
  • Be related to any judge on the panel
  • Have any contract that conflicts with the ambassador role
