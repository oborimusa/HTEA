What is Face Off KWASHATE?

Face Off KWASHATE is the hospitality professionals' challenge within the HTEA 2026 — Kwara Edition. It is not a beauty pageant. It is a skills-based competition that tests the professional excellence of hotel and restaurant staff across Front Office, Housekeeping, and Food & Beverage.

Contestants demonstrate service mastery in real-world scenarios, cooking challenges, and guest-handling tasks — dressed in the uniform of the department they represent.

What It Is — and What It Is Not

It is not a beauty contest. Face Off KWASHATE celebrates skill, professionalism, and service excellence — not appearance.

It is not two titles. There is one winner per state edition: the Face of KWASHATE. One winner. One national representative.

It is not a pageant for the public. It is a professional challenge designed to elevate the standards of Nigeria's hospitality workforce.

It is a platform where the best hotel and restaurant professionals in Kwara State demonstrate why they deserve national recognition.

The Challenge Rounds
Three rounds. One winner per state edition.
Round 1

Service Skills Challenge

Contestants demonstrate core competencies in their department — front office check-in, housekeeping room setup, or F&B table service. Judges score on precision, speed, and guest interaction.

  • Front Office — Check-in & guest relations
  • Housekeeping — Room setup to standard
  • F&B — Table service & order accuracy
Round 2

Cooking & Presentation Challenge

Contestants prepare a signature dish using local Kwara ingredients. They present their dish to the judging panel and explain the cultural story behind it.

  • Signature dish preparation
  • Use of local Kwara ingredients
  • Presentation & cultural storytelling
  • Plating & tasting
Round 3

Guest Scenario Q&A

Contestants handle real-world guest scenarios — complaints, VIP requests, cultural misunderstandings. Judges score on composure, professionalism, and problem-solving.

  • Live scenario response
  • Conflict resolution
  • Cultural awareness
  • Professional communication

How It Works

From application to the crown — four steps.

01

Apply

Hospitality professionals apply through the KWASHATE portal. Employer reference required.

02

Shortlist

Applications are reviewed for eligibility. Shortlisted contestants are notified.

03

Compete

Shortlisted contestants compete across the three challenge rounds on event day.

04

Crowned

Judges announce the Face of KWASHATE. The winner is crowned at the Awards Gala.

👑

The Face of KWASHATE

The winner of Face Off KWASHATE is crowned the Face of KWASHATE — a title that represents excellence in Kwara's hospitality sector.

The Face of KWASHATE becomes an official HTEA ambassador and represents Kwara State at the national final, competing for the title of Face of NIGHATE.

One winner per state edition. One national champion.

Who Can Enter?

Face Off KWASHATE is open to anyone working, studying, or building a career in Kwara State's hospitality, catering, or tourism sector.

Professional

  • Currently employed in a Kwara hotel, restaurant, or tourism business
  • Minimum 1 year of professional experience
  • Employer reference letter required

Student

  • Currently enrolled in a hospitality, catering, tourism, or culinary programme
  • Any Nigerian institution — university, polytechnic, or vocational school
  • Student ID and department letter required

Freelance / Independent

  • Caterers, event chefs, tour guides, or independent hospitality operators
  • Minimum 1 year of active practice
  • Portfolio and one client reference required

All Contestants Must Provide

Documents

  • Valid ID (national, student, or staff)
  • Category-specific proof (see above)

On Event Day

  • Professional uniform (department-specific)
  • One signature dish (Round 2 ingredient list shared after shortlisting)

What We Look For

  • Passion for service excellence
  • Composure under pressure
  • Cultural awareness

Students and professionals compete on the same stage — because excellence has no seniority.
