Apply for Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE 2026

Complete this form to be considered for the 2026 pageant. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until finalists are selected.

📋

Before You Apply

You must be currently employed in Kwara's hospitality or tourism sector. You will need a letter from your employer confirming your role. Read the full pageant page for eligibility and the contestant journey.

What Happens After You Apply

  1. We review your application within 5 working days.
  2. Shortlisted applicants are invited to an in-person audition in Ilorin.
  3. Finalists are notified and invited to the bootcamp.
  4. Finalists compete at the Food Fair and Awards Night.
  5. The two winners are crowned Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE 2026.
