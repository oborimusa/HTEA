Rotating Across Nigeria

HTEA is not a one-off event. It is a national movement. Each year, the awards rotate to a new state — building toward a full national final.

36 States + FCT

One Platform. Every State.

HTEA is designed to rotate across Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. Each state hosts its own edition — producing its own winners, its own Face of the Edition, and its own celebration of hospitality excellence.

Over time, every state in Nigeria will have its own HTEA story, its own cohort of award winners, and its own representative at the national stage.

How the Rotation Works

Each year, HTEA moves to a new host state. Every edition is structured to produce three things: award winners, one Face of the Edition, and a pipeline of talent into the national final.

🏛️

One Host State Per Year

Each edition is anchored in one state, in partnership with local government, hotels, and industry bodies.

🏆

State-Level Awards

Every edition crowns winners across 18 award categories — recognizing the best in that state's hospitality sector.

👑

One Face of the Edition

Face Off — the skills-based challenge — produces one winner per edition: the Face of that edition.

🌍

National Final

Every Face of the Edition competes at the national final for the title of Face of NIGHATE.

🏆 Current Edition

2026 — Kwara State

The inaugural HTEA edition is hosted by Kwara State, under the banner KWASHATE 2026 — the Kwara Edition of the HTEA National Platform. Hosted at Starwood Hotels O2 Arena, Ilorin.

Kwara 2026 will produce 18 award winners and one Face of KWASHATE, who will represent the state at the first national final.

Rotation Roadmap

The confirmed and planned HTEA editions. Additional states will be added each year.

2026

Kwara State — The Inaugural Edition

KWASHATE 2026 launches in Ilorin. 18 award categories. One Face of KWASHATE. The first national finalist in HTEA history.

2027

Osun State — Planned

The second HTEA edition moves to Osun State. A second Face of the Edition will join Kwara's at the national final.

2028

Oyo State — Planned

The third HTEA edition moves to Oyo State, expanding the platform's footprint across the South-West.

2029 →

National Expansion

HTEA expands to additional states across all six geopolitical zones, building toward a full 36-state + FCT national rotation.

👑

Face of NIGHATE

Every state edition produces one Face of the Edition. Those winners converge at the national final — where one emerges as the Face of NIGHATE.

One winner per state. One national champion. A national movement built on merit, not numbers.

The Face of NIGHATE represents Nigeria's hospitality and tourism sector at national and international platforms.
