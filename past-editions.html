Past Editions & Journey

The story of how HTEA came to be — and where it is going next.

Built on Years of Preparation

HTEA is not a new idea. It is the product of years of work in Nigeria's hospitality sector — from concept development and stakeholder engagement to building the operational infrastructure that supports each edition.

Here is the honest history of the platform.

Our Journey

A timeline of the key milestones that led to HTEA 2026.

2019

Concept Development & Stakeholder Engagement

The KWASHATE concept was first developed in 2019. In the course of building the initiative, the founding team held consultative meetings with government stakeholders — including the National Orientation Agency (NOA) and the Office of the then Deputy Governor of Kwara State — to introduce the initiative.

2020 – 2025

Building the Ecosystem

Through Drecot Consults and its divisions — including HospitalityRecruit and Drecot Printing Press — the team built operational infrastructure to support Nigeria's hospitality sector, including staffing, training, and print production. This foundation made a first-class awards platform possible.

2026

HTEA 2026 — The Inaugural Edition

The HTEA platform launches its inaugural edition in Kwara State, under the banner KWASHATE 2026 — the Kwara Edition of the HTEA National Platform. The 2026 edition will deliver the HTEA Gold Award, the Food Fair, and Face Off KWASHATE — relaunched as a fully private-sector-led event.

2027 →

National Rotation

HTEA rotates to a new state each year, building toward a full national final where every edition's Face of the Edition competes for the title of Face of NIGHATE.
