Competition Rules & Criteria

The HTEA Top Hands Challenge is a skills-based competition. We don't judge looks. We judge professionalism, speed, and hospitality excellence.

1. Eligibility & Registration

To ensure fairness and credibility, the following rules apply to all contestants:

  • Employment: All contestants must be currently employed by a registered hotel, restaurant, or catering business in Osun State.
  • Experience: Must have a minimum of 6 months of active experience in their respective department (Front Office, Housekeeping, or F&B).
  • Registration: Contestants must be entered by their employer via the official HTEA Hotel Package. We do not accept walk-in entries on the day of the event.
  • Identification: Contestants must present a valid staff ID or a letter of introduction from their employer on the day of the event.

2. The Challenge Rounds

The competition is divided into two days, testing both practical skills and real-world problem-solving.

Day 1: The Practical Challenge (Friday, April 23)

Contestants will perform live on the main stage in front of an audience and media. They will be tested on:

  • Culinary (For Kitchen/Catering staff): Preparing a signature Osun dish within a strict time limit.
  • Housekeeping (For Housekeeping staff): Bed making to a 5-star standard under time pressure.
  • F&B Service (For Waiters/Front Desk): Table setting, napkin folding, and guest greeting.

Day 2: The Scenario Final (Saturday, April 24)

Only the Top 3 highest-scoring contestants from Day 1 will advance to the Gala Night. They will face a live Q&A with the judges, testing their ability to handle real-life hospitality crises (e.g., handling an angry VIP, dealing with a sudden power outage, or a guest with dietary requirements).

3. Judging & Scoring Matrix

Our judging panel consists of independent industry experts. Scores are based on a strict 100-point system:

Criteria Weight What Judges Look For
Technical Skill 40% Accuracy, speed, hygiene, and standard of the final output.
Presentation & Attitude 20% Uniform, posture, confidence, and communication.
Problem Solving (Q&A) 30% Composure under pressure and common sense in handling scenarios.
Creativity 10% Cultural storytelling (using local ingredients) and unique approaches.

⚖️ Fairness Rule: No contestant will be judged by a manager or owner from their own hotel. Judges must declare any conflict of interest before scoring.

4. Disqualification

A contestant will be immediately disqualified if they:

  • Arrive more than 15 minutes late to their scheduled round.
  • Use pre-prepared ingredients or props that were not approved by the organizers.
  • Show disrespect to judges, organizers, or other contestants.

5. Prizes & Certification

The Champion (1st Place): Trophy for the hotel, ₦50,000 cash, and an automatic slot at the National Finals in Abuja.

The Runners-Up (2nd & 3rd Place): Certificates of excellence and a digital Level 1 HTEA Profile.

The Hotel: The winning hotel receives the "Certified Employer 2027" plaque and priority access to the HospitalityRecruit replacement pool.
