Our Values

The principles that guide HTEA — and the standards we hold ourselves to in every edition.

More Than An Award

HTEA is not just about trophies. It is a platform with a responsibility — to the industry, to the states we work in, and to the people who trust us to recognise excellence fairly. These values are how we honour that responsibility.

Core Values
Six principles that define every HTEA edition.
⚖️

Integrity First

No favouritism. No sponsorship pressure on outcomes. No hidden criteria. Awards are earned, not bought. We will turn down money that comes with strings attached to judging.

🏆

Excellence Without Exception

We celebrate the best, and we hold ourselves to the same standard. From event execution to judging process to communication with nominees, HTEA operates at a level worthy of the industry it honours.

🔍

Transparency

Categories are published. Criteria are public. Judging panels are known. Winners are announced openly. Nothing about HTEA happens behind closed doors.

🇳🇬

National Impact

HTEA rotates across Nigeria's 36 states and the FCT. Every edition leaves behind a stronger hospitality ecosystem, better standards, and a cohort of recognised professionals.

🌍

Cultural Heritage

Nigeria's hospitality sector is built on local culture. HTEA honours that — celebrating cuisine, traditions, and the stories that make Nigerian hospitality distinct.

🤝

Merit Over Seniority

Students and professionals compete on the same stage. A hotel in Kwara gets the same standard of evaluation as a hotel in Lagos. Excellence has no rank, age, or location.

Our Promise to the Industry

We commit to a platform where every nominee is verified, every judge is independent, every vote is counted fairly, and every award is earned.

We commit to publishing the criteria, the panel, and the process — because a platform that cannot be scrutinised cannot be trusted.

We commit to treating every nominee with respect — whether they win or not.

How We Operate

The operating principles behind every HTEA decision.

  • Sponsorship never buys an award. Sponsors support the platform. They do not influence judging. Sponsorship agreements explicitly exclude any award outcome.
  • Judging criteria are published before nominations open. Every nominee knows what they are being scored on.
  • Judges are independent. The National Board and each Edition Judging Panel are drawn from outside the HTEA organisation.
  • Conflicts of interest are declared. Any judge with a personal or business relationship with a nominee must recuse themselves.
  • Voting and scoring are auditable. Public votes and judges' scores are recorded and can be reviewed on request.
  • Winners are announced once. No re-runs, no retractions, unless a verification failure is proven after the event — in which case the award is withdrawn publicly.
  • Nominees are verified before shortlisting. CAC registration, operating years, and category criteria are checked.
  • Communication is respectful. All nominees are treated professionally, win or lose.

Judging & Integrity

The structural safeguards that protect the integrity of every HTEA award.

🏛️ National Board

Permanent custodians of the HTEA standard. Approves categories, oversees every edition, signs off on each local panel, and handles disputes and appeals.

📋 Edition Judging Panel

Each host state assembles its own panel — drawn from hotel associations, government, and the business sector. Local insight, national standards.

🗳️ Public Voting

Public votes carry weight in specific categories but do not determine winners alone. Judges' scores and public votes are combined against published weightings.

📂 Verification

Every nominee is verified for eligibility before shortlisting. False claims of eligibility lead to disqualification.

📜 Dispute Resolution

Any nominee may file a written objection within 7 days of shortlisting. The National Board reviews and responds in writing.

🔒 Data Protection

All nominee and contestant data is handled in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What We Stand Against

HTEA exists partly because these practices are too common in Nigerian awards. We reject them openly.

💰

Pay-to-Win Awards

Where sponsors or nominees can buy their way to a trophy. Not on HTEA. Sponsorship and judging are completely separated.

🎭

Fake Nomination Lists

Where "nominees" are invented to fill a stage. Every HTEA nominee is a real, verified business or individual.

📸

Photo-Op Partnerships

Where government or corporate logos appear on a flyer without any signed agreement. HTEA only lists real, confirmed partners.

🗣️

Unverified Claims

Where event organisers claim attendee numbers, government approval, or media partners they don't have. We publish only what we can prove.

Hold Us to These Standards

If you see HTEA fail to live up to any value on this page, tell us. A platform that cannot be held accountable cannot be trusted.
