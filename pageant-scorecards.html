Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE 2026 — Judge Scorecards

Three official scorecards. One per pillar. Print one set per judge. Total maximum score across all three pillars: 100 points.

Instructions for judges: Score each contestant on every criterion. Do not skip any line. Total each column at the bottom. The final score across all three scorecards determines the winner. Fill in the contestant's name and number at the top of each card before they perform.
