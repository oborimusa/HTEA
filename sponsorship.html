Sponsorship Opportunities

HTEA brings together the decision-makers of Nigeria's hospitality and tourism sector — hotel owners, restaurant managers, travel operators, government officials, and industry media. Sponsorship puts your brand in the room with the people who buy, partner, and invest.

Sponsorship packages are available for the 2026 edition in Kwara State, with national visibility across all future editions.
