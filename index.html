The Face of Nigeria National Cycle

The Face of Nigeria is a continuous, annual search for the country's best hospitality talent. It is not a pageant. It is a rigorous skills-based challenge. Our format spans two calendar years to ensure every state is represented:

Year 1: The State Tours

Over the course of the year, the HTEA team tours all 36 states and the FCT. We host state-level competitions to crown a "Face of [State]" in every single region.

Year 2: The Grand Finale

The following year, the 37 state champions converge in Abuja for the televised Face of Nigeria National Final, where one person is crowned the ultimate Face of Nigeria.

*The platform is always active—while the national final is happening in Abuja, the next state tour is already starting somewhere else.
