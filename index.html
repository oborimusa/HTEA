Judging & Integrity
Two tiers — one permanent standard, one local panel per edition

HTEA operates a two-tier judging system. A permanent National Board sets the standard and oversees every edition. Each host state then assembles its own Edition Judging Panel to score local nominees. This keeps the brand consistent nationally while ensuring judges understand the local market.

How It Works

A transparent, four-step process that ensures fairness.

01

Nomination

Hotels, restaurants, and travel companies self-nominate or get nominated by peers.

02

Verification

Our team verifies eligibility (CAC registration, operating years, etc.).

03

Public Voting

The public votes for their favorites across all categories.

04

Awards Night

Winners are crowned at the prestigious HTEA Gala.
