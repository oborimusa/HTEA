Nigeria's Premier Hospitality Awards

HTEA (Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards) is a national platform that recognizes and celebrates the champions of Nigeria's hospitality and tourism industry. From hotels and restaurants to travel companies and tourism innovators, we honor those who set the standard for excellence.

How It Works

A transparent, four-step process that ensures fairness and celebrates the best.

01

Nomination

Hotels, restaurants, and travel companies self-nominate or get nominated by peers.

02

Verification

Our team verifies eligibility (CAC registration, operating years, etc.).

03

Public Voting

The public votes for their favorites across all categories.

04

Awards Night

Winners are crowned at the prestigious HTEA Gala.
