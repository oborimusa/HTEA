Nigeria's Premier Hospitality Awards

HTEA (Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards) is a national platform that recognizes and celebrates the champions of Nigeria's hospitality and tourism industry. From hotels and restaurants to travel companies and tourism innovators, we honor those who set the standard for excellence.

✨ Our Vision

To become the leading hospitality and tourism awards platform in Nigeria, recognized for celebrating excellence, promoting cultural heritage, and driving economic growth through world-class events.

🎯 Our Mission

To create a prestigious platform that celebrates, rewards, and empowers the champions of Nigeria's hospitality and tourism industry—by providing recognition, networking opportunities, and business growth through an unforgettable blend of awards, entertainment, and cultural celebration.
