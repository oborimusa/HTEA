The Official 2-Day Program

From the heat of the live cooking challenge to the glamour of the black-tie gala. Here is exactly how HTEA Osun 2027 will unfold.

📖 View Competition Rules
Day 1

Friday, April 23, 2027

The Food Fair & Live Skills Challenge

10:00 AM

Gates Open: The Osun Food Fair

The public exhibition begins. 20 selected local vendors open their stalls to sell authentic Osun delicacies. Live music and entertainment set the atmosphere.

  • Open to the public and media.
  • Sponsor booths and brand activations are live.
11:30 AM

🔥 The Live Skills Challenge (70% of Score)

This is the main event of the day. The 10 shortlisted hospitality staff compete live on the main stage in front of the crowd and judges. No private rooms. Real pressure. Real skills.

  • Round 1: Culinary (Preparing a signature Osun dish).
  • Round 2: Housekeeping (5-star bed making under time constraints).
  • Round 3: F&B (Table setting and napkin folding).
2:30 PM

Judges Deliberation & Vendor Sales

The judges compile the scores. The Food Fair continues as the public enjoys the delicacies. Contestants take a break and prepare for the Gala.

4:00 PM

Announcement of the Top 3 Finalists

Only the Top 3 highest-scoring contestants are announced. They will advance to the Gala Night for the final Q&A round. The rest receive their digital Level 1 verification profiles.

Day 2

Saturday, April 24, 2027

The Gala & Awards Night

6:00 PM

Red Carpet & VIP Reception

Hotel GMs, owners, government officials, and sponsors arrive in black-tie. Networking session and photo opportunities with the media.

7:00 PM

The 8 Excellence Awards

We honour the owners and operators who have built Osun's hospitality industry. We recognize excellence across Osogbo, Ife, Ilesa/Ede zones, and more.

  • Category Sponsors present the awards on stage.
  • Winners receive the "Certified Employer 2027" plaque.
8:15 PM

👑 The Top Hand Final (30% of Score)

The Top 3 contestants from Friday return to the stage. The judges test them on real-life hospitality scenarios (e.g., handling an angry VIP, dealing with a sudden power outage, or a guest with dietary requirements).

  • Live Q&A with the judges.
  • Final combined score is calculated (Friday 70% + Saturday 30%).
8:45 PM

The Crowning of the Top Hand

One winner is crowned the Osun Top Hand 2027. They receive a cash prize, national recognition, and an automatic slot at the Abuja National Finals. Their hotel receives the grand trophy.

9:15 PM

Closing Remarks & After-Party

The Founder of Drecot Consults gives the closing speech. The night ends with entertainment and high-level networking.
