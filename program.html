Competition Rules & Criteria

The HTEA Top Hands Challenge is a skills-based competition. We don't judge looks. We judge professionalism, speed, and hospitality excellence.

1. Eligibility & Registration

To ensure fairness and credibility, the competition is divided into two distinct divisions for the Day 1 qualifiers:

Division A: The Professional Division

  • Employment: Must be currently employed by a registered hotel, restaurant, or catering business in Osun State.
  • Experience: Minimum of 6 months of active experience in their respective department.
  • Registration: Entered by their employer via the official HTEA Hotel Package.

Division B: The Student & Practitioner Division

  • Students: Currently enrolled in a hospitality, catering, tourism, or culinary program. Student ID required.
  • Freelancers/Practitioners: Independent caterers, private chefs, or tour guides operating in Osun State.
  • Registration: Can register individually via the Student/Practitioner entry ticket.

2. The Challenge Rounds

The competition is divided into two days, testing both practical skills and real-world problem-solving.

Day 1: The Practical Challenge (Friday, April 23)

Contestants will perform live on the main stage in front of an audience and media. They will be tested on:

  • Culinary (For Kitchen/Catering staff): Preparing a signature Osun dish within a strict time limit.
  • Housekeeping (For Housekeeping staff): Bed making to a 5-star standard under time pressure.
  • F&B Service (For Waiters/Front Desk): Table setting, napkin folding, and guest greeting.

Day 2: The Grand Finale (Saturday, April 24)

The Top 3 from the Professional Division and the Top 3 from the Student Division advance to the Gala Night. They now compete against each other for ONE single title.

They will face a live Q&A with the judges, testing their ability to handle real-life hospitality crises (e.g., handling an angry VIP, dealing with a sudden power outage, or a guest with dietary requirements).

3. Judging & Scoring Matrix

Our judging panel consists of independent industry experts. Scores are based on a strict 100-point system:

Criteria Weight What Judges Look For
Technical Skill (Day 1) 40% Accuracy, speed, hygiene, and standard of the final output.
Presentation & Attitude (Day 1) 20% Uniform, posture, confidence, and communication.
Problem Solving (Day 2 Q&A) 30% Composure under pressure and common sense in handling scenarios.
Creativity 10% Cultural storytelling (using local ingredients) and unique approaches.

⚖️ Fairness Rule: No contestant will be judged by a manager or owner from their own hotel or school. Judges must declare any conflict of interest before scoring.

4. Disqualification

A contestant will be immediately disqualified if they:

  • Arrive more than 15 minutes late to their scheduled round.
  • Use pre-prepared ingredients or props that were not approved by the organizers.
  • Show disrespect to judges, organizers, or other contestants.

5. Prizes & The Single Winner Model

The Divisional Winners (Day 1): The highest scorer in the Professional Division and the highest scorer in the Student Division will both receive recognition and Level 1 Digital Certificates.

The Grand Champion (Day 2): The Top 3 from each division advance to the Grand Finale. They compete in a unified Q&A round. The final score is calculated (Friday 70% + Saturday 30%). The highest overall scorer, regardless of their division, is crowned the Face of Osun Hospitality 2027.

  • The Champion: Trophy for their hotel/school, ₦50,000 cash, and the sole slot to represent Osun at the Abuja National Finals.
  • First Runners-Up: Certificates and digital profiles.
