About HTEA

HTEA is Nigeria's premier awards platform celebrating the best in hospitality and tourism. Each year, the event rotates to a new state, creating a national movement of excellence.

Our Story

HTEA (Hospitality & Tourism Excellence Awards) was born out of a simple observation: Nigeria's hospitality and tourism industry is filled with incredible talent, hardworking businesses, and innovative ideas—but these champions were not being recognized.

We created HTEA to change that. It is a national platform that celebrates, rewards, and empowers the best in the industry. Each year, the awards rotate to a new state, ensuring that every corner of Nigeria gets the opportunity to shine.

From hotel owners and restaurant managers to travel companies and tourism innovators, HTEA honors those who set the standard for excellence. This is not just an awards show— it is a movement.

✨ Our Vision

To become the leading hospitality and tourism awards platform in Nigeria, recognized for celebrating excellence, promoting cultural heritage, and driving economic growth through world-class events.

🎯 Our Mission

To create a prestigious platform that celebrates, rewards, and empowers the champions of Nigeria's hospitality and tourism industry—by providing recognition, networking opportunities, and business growth through an unforgettable blend of awards, entertainment, and cultural celebration.

Why HTEA Exists

  • 🏆 To Recognize Excellence: We celebrate the businesses and individuals who have gone above and beyond to deliver world-class hospitality and tourism experiences.
  • 🌟 To Celebrate Culture: We create unforgettable experiences that honor Nigeria's rich cultural heritage, from local cuisine to traditional music.
  • 🤝 To Build Networks: We connect industry leaders, government officials, sponsors, and entrepreneurs to foster collaboration and growth.
  • 📈 To Drive Economic Growth: By boosting the hospitality sector, we create jobs, attract investment, and contribute to the prosperity of host states.

Rotating Across Nigeria

HTEA is not a one-off event. It is a national movement. Each year, the awards rotate to a new state.
