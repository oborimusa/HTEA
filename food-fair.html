The HTEA Food Fair

A celebration of Nigerian cuisine, local vendors, and culinary excellence — hosted at every HTEA edition.

Current Edition: KWASHATE 2026 — Kwara State

Where Nigeria Eats Together

The HTEA Food Fair is a full-day celebration of Nigerian food, culture, and culinary talent. Restaurants, caterers, food entrepreneurs, and vendors come together to exhibit, sell, and compete — while the Face Off contestant cooking challenge unfolds on the main stage.

Open to the public. Open to the industry. A platform for the businesses that feed Nigeria.

What Happens at the Food Fair
Four reasons the HTEA Food Fair draws a crowd.
🍽️

Vendor Stalls

Restaurants, caterers, and food entrepreneurs set up booths to showcase and sell signature dishes to the public, industry guests, and press.

👨‍🍳

Live Cooking Competition

Face Off KWASHATE contestants prepare signature dishes using local Kwara ingredients, judged live on stage by the edition's panel.

🌾

Local Ingredients

A dedicated section showcasing Kwara's agricultural produce, spices, and indigenous ingredients — connecting farmers and producers with the food industry.

🎤

Entertainment & Culture

Live music, cultural performances, tastings, and family-friendly activities throughout the day. The Food Fair is open to the public — not just industry guests.

Who Can Exhibit?

The HTEA Food Fair welcomes businesses across Nigeria's food and beverage ecosystem.

🍔 Restaurants & Fast-Food

Fine dining, casual restaurants, fast-food chains, and quick-service operators looking to reach new customers.

👩‍🍳 Caterers & Event Chefs

Independent caterers, private chefs, and event catering businesses showcasing their signature menus.

🍰 Food Entrepreneurs

Bakeries, confectioners, packaged food producers, and specialty food brands.

🌾 Farmers & Producers

Local farmers, spice producers, and ingredient suppliers connecting directly with the food industry.

🥤 Beverage Brands

Non-alcoholic drinks, juices, water brands, cocktails, and specialty beverages. (Alcoholic brands require separate agreement.)

🎓 Culinary Schools

Hospitality, catering, and culinary institutions showcasing student talent and programme offerings.

Programme Highlights

The Food Fair runs from morning to evening — an all-day celebration that closes with the Awards Gala the following evening.

🌅

Morning — Vendor Set-Up & Public Opening

Vendors arrive, booths are set up, and doors open to the public. Guests begin arriving from mid-morning.

☀️

Afternoon — Live Cooking Challenge

Face Off KWASHATE contestants compete on the main stage. Judging, tasting, and audience engagement.

🌆

Evening — Cultural Performances & Networking

Live music, cultural showcases, and open networking for industry guests, sponsors, and the public.

🎉

Next Day — Awards Gala

The Food Fair flows into the HTEA Gold Awards Night — where Food Fair vendors are eligible for catering and culinary award categories.
