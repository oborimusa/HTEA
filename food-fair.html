KWASHATE Food Fair

A celebration of Nigerian cuisine and Kwara's local food vendors. Restaurants, caterers, and food entrepreneurs exhibit, sell, and compete — while the pageant's live cooking challenge unfolds on stage.

Register as a Vendor What to Expect

Food is the gateway to culture — and Kwara has one of Nigeria's richest culinary traditions. The KWASHATE Food Fair brings together restaurants, caterers, food entrepreneurs, and home cooks for a full day of tasting, exhibition, and networking.

Vendors get real exposure to hotel owners, restaurateurs, tourism operators, and media — the exact audience that can turn a local food business into a household name.

What Happens at the Fair
One day. Multiple activations. Open to the public.
🍲

Vendor Stalls

Restaurants, caterers, and food entrepreneurs exhibit and sell signature dishes to a live audience.

🔥

Live Cooking Challenge

Mr. & Mrs. KWASHATE contestants compete in "The Heat" — a sealed-basket cooking challenge on stage.

🥗

Local Ingredient Showcase

Highlighting the produce, spices, and local ingredients that make Kwara cuisine distinct.

🤝

B2B Networking

Hotel F&B managers, restaurateurs, and caterers meet suppliers and collaborators.

Vendor Requirements

What KWASHATE Provides

  • Booth space with basic structure
  • Table and chairs per package tier
  • Electricity point access
  • Event program listing
  • Security and crowd management
  • Waste disposal

What You Must Bring

  • Your own cooking equipment (per your menu)
  • Food handling and hygiene compliance
  • NAFDAC compliance for packaged goods
  • Branding materials (banner, signboard)
  • Staff uniform for your team
  • Change of cash / POS terminal
