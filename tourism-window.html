KWASHATE 2026 is Officially Listed

KWASHATE 2026 — the Kwara State edition of HTEA — has been submitted to Nigeria's National Digital Events and Experiences Calendar. It will be discoverable by Nigerians, the diaspora, and international visitors throughout the 2026 tourism season.

Visit KWASHATE 2026 → View on National Calendar

Part of Ember to Remember 2026

HTEA and KWASHATE 2026 are part of Nigeria's 100-day national tourism season (September–December 2026), a federal initiative designed to connect culture, tourism, hospitality, and the creative economy into one coordinated national experience.

The initiative brings together festivals, awards, exhibitions, and cultural events across the country, positioning Nigeria as a destination for diaspora return, international tourism, and domestic discovery.

By participating, HTEA strengthens its alignment with the federal tourism strategy — giving sponsors and partners direct visibility on a national stage.
