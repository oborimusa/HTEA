The Tourism Window

HTEA's showcase of Nigeria's tourism destinations, attractions, and cultural heritage.

Every Edition Opens a Window

Each HTEA edition spotlights the host state's tourism ecosystem — its destinations, attractions, cultural sites, and the businesses that make travel in Nigeria worth it. This is the Tourism Window.

It is not a trade show. It is a national showcase of what makes each state a destination — and a platform for tourism operators to be recognised, connected, and celebrated.

What the Tourism Window Does

The Tourism Window connects three audiences: tourism operators, industry decision-makers, and the public. It does this by giving each edition a dedicated platform to showcase the host state's tourism assets.

🗺️

Destination Showcase

Hotels, resorts, tour operators, and attractions exhibit at a dedicated section of each edition — putting their services in front of industry buyers and the public.

🏛️

Cultural Heritage

Local heritage sites, festivals, and cultural experiences are featured — connecting cultural custodians with the tourism industry that can bring visitors to them.

🤝

B2B Connections

Tourism operators meet travel agencies, corporate event planners, and hospitality decision-makers — creating partnerships that extend beyond the event.

🏆

Award Recognition

Tourism operators compete for dedicated categories — including Best Tour Operator, Best Local Tourist Attraction, and Best Sustainable Tourism Initiative.

What the Window Offers
Four ways the Tourism Window creates value for host states and operators.
📣

State Spotlight

Every edition puts the host state's tourism assets on a national platform — through digital features, press coverage, and on-site showcases that reach decision-makers across Nigeria.

🎤

Speaking Platform

Tourism operators, cultural custodians, and state tourism officials are given stage time at each edition to present their destination to an audience of industry leaders and the public.

🛍️

Market Access

Tourism businesses connect directly with the buyers — travel agencies, corporate planners, and hospitality managers — who can fill their rooms, tours, and venues.

🌱

Sustainable Tourism

A dedicated category recognises tourism operators who build responsibly — protecting Nigeria's natural and cultural assets while growing the industry.

How It Works

Four steps from application to national recognition.

01

Apply

Tourism operators, hotels, attractions, and cultural sites apply for the Tourism Window showcase.

02

Verify

Our team verifies eligibility — business registration, operating history, and category criteria.

03

Showcase

Verified participants exhibit at the event and pitch their destination to industry guests and the public.

04

Get Recognised

Winners in tourism categories are crowned at the Awards Gala, and featured across HTEA platforms.

Who Can Participate?

The Tourism Window is open to businesses, institutions, and cultural custodians.

🏨 Hospitality & Lodging

Hotels, resorts, guest houses, and short-stay accommodations.

🎯 Tour Operators

Local tour companies, guided experience providers, and destination management companies.

🏛️ Cultural Sites & Festivals

Heritage sites, cultural festivals, museums, and traditional institutions.

🚌 Travel & Transport

Airlines, transport operators, car rentals, and travel agencies serving the tourism sector.

🌿 Nature & Eco-Tourism

National parks, nature reserves, and sustainable tourism initiatives.

🎨 Creative & Cultural

Cultural performers, craftspeople, and creative businesses that support tourism experiences.

2026 — Kwara State Tourism Window

The inaugural Tourism Window opens at KWASHATE 2026 — the Kwara Edition of HTEA. Kwara State's tourism assets will be showcased to a national audience, including industry leaders, media, and government officials.

From the Owú Waterfalls to the Esie Museum, from Ilorin's cultural heritage to the state's growing hospitality sector — Kwara's tourism story will be told at a national level.

Bring Your Destination to the Window

If you run a tourism business, cultural site, or hospitality destination in Kwara State, the Tourism Window is your platform. Reach out to learn how to participate.
