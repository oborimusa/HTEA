The HTEA HR System: Certify. Retain. Replace.

The only talent management system built for Nigerian hotels. We test your staff at our live Expo, track them with a digital QR code, and if they leave, our agency (HospitalityRecruit) instantly supplies a verified replacement.

How The Ecosystem Works
We solve the "Train & Run" problem using the prestige of an expo, the power of tech, and the safety of an agency.
1

Certify (The Expo)

At the HTEA Osun Expo, your staff undergoes a live skills challenge in front of judges and media. We test them as they are—no extra training. They don't just win an award; they get a digital QR profile that proves their skills.

2

Retain (The Tech)

We issue a digital QR code tied to your hotel. If they stay 6 months, you upgrade them to Gold. At 12 months, Platinum. The staff wants to stay because the higher badge unlocks national HTEA finals and 5-star job placements.

3

Replace (The Agency)

If a competitor poaches them anyway, their certificate is instantly voided on the platform. But you don't lose. You log into HospitalityRecruit, and we instantly supply a pre-vetted, Level 1 replacement.

Powered by the HTEA Verified Platform

We don't use paper certificates that can be faked or ignored. We use a real-time digital QR code system.

Any employer or guest can scan a staff member's badge. The system instantly checks the database to see if they are still valid, who they work for, and their Tier Level.

  • Active: Staff is currently employed at your hotel.
  • Voided: Staff has left. The certificate cannot be used for a new job.
  • Gold/Platinum: Staff has stayed longer, proving their loyalty and skill.

This protects your investment. The staff cannot use your hotel's training to jump to a competitor without starting over.
